# Awesome GEO — AI Search Monitoring & Optimization

> A curated list of tools, frameworks, and resources for **Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)** —
> monitoring how AI search engines and LLM answer engines cite, rank, and describe your brand,
> and optimizing your content so those answers are accurate and favorable.

Traditional SEO optimizes for a ranked list of links. GEO optimizes for a **synthesized answer**:
the model decides what to retrieve, what to quote, and whom to credit. That shift changes what
you measure — not just ranking position, but citation share, mention sentiment, and answer accuracy.
